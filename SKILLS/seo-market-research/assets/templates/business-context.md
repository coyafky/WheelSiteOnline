# Business Context

## Verified Scope

Record only claims supported by first-party business evidence.

## Product Lines

For each line, record verified offerings, processes, applications, terminology, exclusions, and evidence IDs. Keep all four lines structurally parallel.

## Proof Inventory

List available and missing proof. A requested certification, specification, capacity figure, customer name, or performance claim is not proof that the business has it.

## Constraints

- No public prices.
- Inquiry service owns inquiry data.
- Different wheel manufacturing processes identify separate products linked through a design series.

## Evidence Gaps

List the business questions that must be resolved before query or page decisions can be trusted.
