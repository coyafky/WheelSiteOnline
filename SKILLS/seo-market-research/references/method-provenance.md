## Cross-Source Decisions

- `KEEP`: provenance, explicit degradation, raw/normalized separation, localized SERPs, page ownership, typed competitors, and structured handoff.
- `ADAPT`: persistent profiles, collector/synthesis separation, buyer stages, scoring components, and clustering thresholds.
- `REJECT`: fabricated fallback metrics, fixed content ratios, universal volume cutoffs, unqualified connected components, keyword-to-page automation, generic ranking timelines, and uncalibrated composite scores.

For file-level evidence, snapshot hashes, and detailed assessments, use `research/seo-skill-study/sources.md` and the 19 cards in `research/seo-skill-study/skills/`.
