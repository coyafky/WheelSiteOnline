# Page Mapping And Information Architecture

## Ownership States

Assign each reviewed cluster `EXISTING_PAGE`, `NEW_PAGE_CANDIDATE`, `SECTION`, `SUPPORTING_ASSET`, `DEFER`, `DO_NOT_TARGET`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

One cluster is not automatically one URL. A candidate requires search evidence, verified business fit, a buyer job, observed page type, proof requirements, and a valid inquiry path.

## Page Map Record

Include cluster, ownership state, page type, working title, proposed URL when appropriate, parent candidate, product line, buyers/jobs, primary/secondary queries, link targets, CTA class, proof requirements, fit states, sources, claim type, confidence, and decision notes.

CTA classes are `EDUCATION`, `TECHNICAL_VALIDATION`, `SUPPLIER_QUALIFICATION`, `PRODUCT_INQUIRY`, `GENERAL_INQUIRY`, or `NONE`. Do not publish prices.

## IA Reconciliation

Reconcile approved candidates/content types, buyer mental models, four parallel lines, stable URL constraints, product/process identity, navigation/parents/cross-links/taxonomy, orphan/duplication/cannibalization checks, and future card-sort/tree-test/first-click validation.

Search demand may justify deeper coverage inside one line but cannot make it the structural parent of the others.

## Page-Brief Handoff

A brief input is research, not copy. Include observed intent/page-type distribution, buyers/jobs, query evidence and provenance, competing result patterns, sourced questions/entities, proof requirements, unavailable claims, internal relationships, CTA class, open questions, and prohibited claims.

Do not write or implement the page within this skill.
