# Market, Buyers, And Competitors

## Market And Offering Truth

Inventory verified products, manufacturing processes, capabilities, applications, materials, proof assets, terminology, exclusions, and unresolved claims before query generation.

For each entity record the approved label and sourced aliases, type/product-line ownership, `VERIFIED`/`HYPOTHESIS`/`NOT_OFFERED`/`UNKNOWN` status, supporting and contradictory evidence, required proof, and product/process relationships.

Do not infer a capability from a filename, image, competitor claim, or search demand.

## Buyer Roles And Buying Jobs

Treat supplied personas as hypotheses. Research roles, organization context, triggers, requirements, objections, proof sought, information handoffs, and RFQ needs.

Useful nonlinear buying jobs include problem identification, solution exploration, requirements building, supplier discovery/selection, technical validation, commercial/risk validation, consensus/procurement, and RFQ preparation.

For industrial research, explicitly investigate drawing/specification vocabulary, materials/processes, standards, tolerances, application constraints, quality/audit evidence, sampling/prototyping, capacity/logistics questions, and supplier qualification. These are research targets, not claims that the business supports them.

## Competitor Roles

Classify observed entities by role:

- `BUSINESS`: substitutable offering to similar buyers;
- `SERP`: repeatedly ranks for the query set;
- `CONTENT`: owns useful technical/educational coverage;
- `MARKETPLACE_DIRECTORY`: intermediates discovery/comparison;
- `FORUM_COMMUNITY`: satisfies experiential/validation needs;
- `STANDARD_REFERENCE`: authoritative technical/regulatory material.

Also classify market position as `DIRECT`, `ADJACENT`, `ASPIRATIONAL`, `SMALLER_SPECIALIST`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Store query/page evidence for every label. User confirmation is especially important for `BUSINESS`.
