# Business Opportunity And Buyer Jobs

## Opportunity Vector

Assess each reviewed cluster on buyer fit, verified product/capability fit, commercial/qualification intent, measured demand and coverage limits, SERP winnability, strategic authority, inquiry potential, evidence confidence, and effort/dependency.

Every component contains a label or configured score, reason, confidence, and source IDs. Do not issue a composite until weights and rubrics are approved. Low-volume RFQ-oriented queries may outrank high-volume information queries when business fit and conversion path are stronger.

Use `RESEARCH_NOW`, `CANDIDATE`, `DEFER`, `DO_NOT_TARGET`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

## Hard Stops

Stop page candidacy when the offering is unsupported, required proof is unavailable, product/process identity is unresolved, decisive SERP evidence is missing/stale, the conversion path violates project boundaries, or the opportunity depends on unauthorized/unstable collection.

## Buyer-Job Mapping

After clustering, map clusters back to buyer roles and nonlinear jobs. Initial personas may be disproved by observed queries and SERPs.

Record buyer hypotheses/context, jobs, trigger/need, next helpful action, required proof, current coverage/gap, contradictions, sources, and confidence. Do not force TOFU/MOFU/BOFU if it hides procurement, engineering validation, consensus, or RFQ work.
